Puggy Bot Realm Commands

A complete and organized list of all commands available for usage in the Puggy Realm. None of these commands are available for the RK Skyblock. IMPORTANT: If you are looking for the full list of commands, go to Puggy Commands. If you are looking for Puggy Bot External commands, go to Puggy External Commands.

Realm Commands

!quicktp

Teleports you to a room where there are npc to teleport anywhere in the Realm!

!kit

Shows you a list of kits you can claim using commands

!event-kit

Gives you the latest even kit

!food-kit

Gives you a kit full of food items to prevent you from starving

!starter-kit

Gives you a starter kit to begin your journey in the Realm

!credits

Shows you the credits for the Realm

!lag

Kills all unimportant entities to remove lag

!shareplot and !removeplotaccess

Allows you to share your plot with any user of your choice.

Allows you to remove someones access from your plot

Note: If your plot is in the old system it will not work. You will need to contact staff to move your plot to the new system.

!tpa, tpaccept, and tpdeny

Allows you to send a teleport request to another user, accept a teleport request, or deny a teleport request.

!particle, !particlepurchase, and !removeparticle

Allows you to enable or disable either one of your particle effects

Allows you to purchase a particle effect from the shop

Allows you to remove your particle effect to allow you to purchase another

!emote, !emotepurchase, and !removeemote

Allows you to enable or disable either one of your emotes

Allows you to purchase an emote from the shop

Allows you to remove your emote to allow you to purchase another

Specific Rank Commands

!apple

Gives people with the Apple rank one stack of apples, golden apples, and enchanted golden apples
