Puggy Bot Commands

A complete and organized list of all commands available in the Puggy Bot. IMPORTANT: If you are looking for /chatbot or other external commands, go to Puggy External Commands. If you are looking for Puggy Realm commands, go to Puggy Realm Commands.

Fun/Doog Commands

/doog

Sends a random photo of the Doog.

/doogbomb

Sends a Doog photo to another user, with optional hints.

DM Required Hint Option

/doogquote

Sends a quote said by the Doog.

/xtictactoe

Play Tic-Tac-Toe against the bot.

/xblackjack

Play Blackjack against the bot or another player.

/secret

Enter a code to view a secret put into the bot.

Pug Points

/buildings

Buy buildings that generate Pug Points over time.

Mineshaft Factory Bank

/boosts

Purchase upgrades to improve building efficiency.

/scoreboard

View leaderboards for a variety of different stats.

Utility

/botinfo

Displays a list of commands and more information about the bot.

/status

Shows which bot systems are online or offline.

/terms

Sends a reminder of the Puggy Bot Terms of Service.
