Puggy Bot Commands

A complete and organized list of all commands available in the Puggy Bot. IMPORTANT: If you are looking for /chatbot or other external commands, go to Puggy External Commands. If you are looking for Puggy Realm commands, go to Puggy Realm Commands.

Fun/Doog Commands

/doog

Sends a random photo of the Doog.

1-5 Photos

/doogbomb

Sends a Doog photo to another user, with optional hints.

DM Required Hint Option 1-5 Photos

/doogquote

Sends a quote said by the Doog.

/xtictactoe

Play Tic-Tac-Toe against the bot or another player.

Betting (Integer option)

/xblackjack

Play Blackjack against the bot or another player.

Betting (Integer option)

/secret

Enter a code to view a secret put into the bot.

Pug Points

/buildings

Buy buildings that generate Pug Points over time.

Mineshaft Factory Bank

/rig

Send your samurai pugs to raid other users buildings and steal their points.

/boosts

Allows you to purchase boosts that change your buildings in beneficial ways.

/quests

View and complete daily, weekly, and monthly quests for random rewards.

/crates

Allows you to claim daily, weekly, and monthly crates for rewards and points.

/craft

Allows you to craft items required to make buildings.

/gather

Allows you to gather resources and manage your inventory.

/pug-search

Allows you to search for Pugs and add them to your collection.

Search Collection

/leaderboard

View leaderboards for a variety of different stats.

Utility

/botinfo

Displays the general information about the bot and all of its commands. It also displays the current status of each command, showing if the commands are functioning or experiencing issues.

/terms

Sends a reminder of the Puggy Bot Terms of Service.

/settings

Allows you to customize your preferences and settings for the bot.
