Puggy Bot External Commands

A complete and organized list of all commands available for external usage in the Puggy Bot. IMPORTANT: If you are looking for the full list of commands, go to Puggy Commands. If you are looking for Puggy Realm commands, go to Puggy Realm Commands.

Externally Usable Commands

/doog

Sends a photo of the Doog.

/doogbomb

Sends a Doog photo to a user with an optional hint.

/doogquote

Sends a quote said by the Doog.

/scoreboard

Shows global or local score/leaderboards having stats from many commands.

/secret

Enter a secret code to unlock hidden content.

/xtictactoe

Play tic-tac-toe against the bot.

/xblackjack

Play blackjack against either the bot or another user with custom made cards.

/terms

Shows the Puggy Bot terms of service.

/help

Shows bot usage help and commands.
