Puggy Realm Plots

Learn everything about plots and what you can do with them.

What Are Plots?

Free Build Areas:

Plots are free 64×64 areas of land where you can build anything you want. You can claim a plot by pressing the "Want a plot" button in the plot area or by asking in the Discord server.

Note: Specially named plots are a plot upgrade in the normal shop. A green color comes along with it for your sign.

Security System:

Each plot teleports unauthorized users back to the hub automatically.

Sharing Access:

Give others access using !shareplot and remove with !removeplotaccess.

Rank Plot Buffs

Staff

Staff members can claim infinite plots and build freely, including using creative mode.

Vip+

Vip+ rank gives free plot upgrades, a mailbox, half-size basement, carpet on border blocks, and a purple TP sign color.

Clan Plots

Clan plots come with a free clan name and can be accessed by all clan members. Clan members can upgrade the plot and have a carpet cover over border blocks for decoration.

2x & 4x Plots

Expanded plots: 2x (128x128) and 4x (256x256). Upgrades available in the normal shop.

Opened / Shop Plots

Accessible to all players, typically used for shops or showcases.
