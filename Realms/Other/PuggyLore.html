Realm Lore

This page documents the lore of the Puggy Realm. The lore is the story that explains the history and currently storyline of the Puggy Realm. This lore is NOT related to the RK Skyblock in any way. This lore is not official, it was written by a member who has dedicated a lot of time into researching the Realm Lore through the hints and information we have given over time. It is not complete.

Written by: hobby_0133. on Discord (Saria)

PERIOD ONE — The Ancient Roofus Doofus

There were five Roofus Doofus gods. The fifth one, The Doomed Doofus, was banished from the group with the other four. Soon later they were reincarnated as wolves and were unable to use their full power. They took over the land known as the "Main World" once reincarnated.

PERIOD TWO — The Goobers

A new group of wolves appeared, the Goobers. They were a group of Doofus (not the gods) who left the group. They started a war with the Doofus called RB-Goob W1. After a peace treaty, the king and queen were granted the same authority as the four reincarnated gods, forming six leaders of the Main World.

PERIOD THREE — Swedia

A new world was introduced called Swedia. Managed by a king and queen, they created an investigation corporation known as the W.C.A. Researchers including Power and Sour were sent to the Main World, where they encountered the Roofus Doofus and entered a conflict called the World Doofus War 1 (WDW1).

PERIOD FOUR — Swedia's Own Conflict

Power and Sour returned to Swedia to find it in turmoil. A revolution had begun surrounding Sour’s growing power. One side feared her influence, while the other believed she posed no threat. Sour is known to be a godlike entity similar to the Roofus Doofus, though much about her remains unknown.

PERIOD FIVE — Refugees

As Swedia’s conflict worsened, civilians began fleeing to the Main World aboard official W.C.A. ships. The only confirmed vessel was the one used by Sour and Power during their second investigation. During this period, they encountered Sen4♦dv2Ê, whose actions resulted in the creation of evil forms of people and an attempted world takeover.

PERIOD SIX — Cheese Sphere / Moon

Sen4♦dv2Ê pulled the moon from the sky and placed his power source atop it, transforming it into a battle zone between the people and their evil counterparts. The war ended when the top of the sphere was nuked. Though it was believed Sour and Power died in the blast, they survived.

PERIOD SEVEN — The Barracks

After the moon war, another sphere collided with the destroyed surface. Known as the Barracks, it was formed from Sen4♦dv2Ê’s green power substance. Inside were thousands of entities called Bariks, created to fight alongside him and amplify his power.
