Private Smelters

What Are Private Smelters?

Personal/Private Rooms:

Private Smelters are personal rooms where you can smelt, craft, and manage items without interference.

Every room includes:

Furnaces (and blast furnaces), smokers, crafting tables, chests, spots to place kits, and lava disposals.

Another purpose:

You can let others in your room to trade items with them or let them take items from a kit without the risk of unwanted players taking the items.
