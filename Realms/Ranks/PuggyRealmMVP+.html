MVP+ Rank Area

MVP+ unlocks additional high paying gens, obsidian showers, and exclusive kit features not available in MVP or lower ranks.

MVP+ Zone Preview

What's Inside?

  • Obsidian Shower & New Generators

    Five new gens: element 69, element 92, empty undyed shulker boxes, tuff, and full Puggy God Kits. Includes an obsidian shower similar to the netherite shower in MVP.

  • New Sell Gens & Free Mayfly

    Two new sell gens, "Your Life" and element 69, plus a button to claim a mayfly for free at any time.

  • Other Features

    Large kit placing area next to the obsidian shower, music disc box and jukebox for relaxing, black room with chests and beds, plus the entrance to the MVP++ zone.

How to Obtain MVP+

MVP+ can be purchased in the Rank Shop. The entrance is located in the MVP zone.
