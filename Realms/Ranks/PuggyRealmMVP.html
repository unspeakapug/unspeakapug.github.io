What's Inside?

  • Automatic Netherite shower

    Fully automated Netherite ingot shower that provides a steady stream of Netherite directly on top of you.

  • New gens

    Ten new gens are in this area. Bookshelves, beacons, netherrack, crying obsidian, obsidian, grass, glass, magma, sea lanterns, and shroomlights. Out of all these, obsidian and crying obsidian are the only ones able to be sold, for $2,650 and $1,750.

  • Large kit area and storage zone

    There is one large kit placing area as well as many barrels on the other side of the room for you to dump your unwanted items.

How to Obtain MVP++

MVP++ can be purchased in the Rank Shop. The entrance is located in the MVP+ zone.
