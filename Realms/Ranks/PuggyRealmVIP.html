VIP Rank Area

The VIP Rank Area provides exclusive gens, sellers, and progression access unavailable to others.

VIP Zone Preview

What’s Inside?

  • Exclusive Gens & Sellers

    Access all ore block generators and totem and arrow sellers. Totems sell for $5,000 each and arrows for $100.

  • Rank Progression Access

    Inside the storage room is the entrance to the MVP rank area, granting access to all higher ranks once purchased.

How to Obtain VIP

VIP can be purchased in the Rank Shop for $700,000. The VIP entrance is located inside a large portal within the hub.
