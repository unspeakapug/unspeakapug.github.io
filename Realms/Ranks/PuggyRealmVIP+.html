VIP+ Rank Area

VIP+ is the second smallest rank area while still letting you unlock experimental features, new/powerful generators, and unique blocks not available anywhere else.

VIP+ Zone Preview

What’s Inside?

  • Unknown Element Gen & Seller

    Access the exclusive Unknown Element gen and seller. Each sells for $5,000.

  • Kit Duper & Infinite Animals

    Use the kit duper alongside a regenerating kit filled with mob spawn eggs you cannot get anywhere else.

  • VIP++ and experimental features

    Access to VIP++ (You still must have the role) and future experimental features as they are released.

How to Obtain VIP+

VIP+ can be purchased in the Rank Shop. The entrance is located inside the MVP++ Zone.
