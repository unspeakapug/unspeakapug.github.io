MVP++ Rank Area

MVP++ is by far the smallest primary rank area in the Puggy Realm with only 10 duplicators and no other features.

MVP++ Zone Preview

What's Inside?

  • Duplicators

    In the Mvp++ area, there are 10 different duplicators that can duplicate almost any block and item in the game that you place in it.

How to Obtain MVP++

MVP++ can be purchased in the Rank Shop. The entrance is located in the MVP+ Zone.
