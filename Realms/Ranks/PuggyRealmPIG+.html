PIG+ Rank Area

Pig+ is the biggest and most providing rank area in the Puggy Realm. It includes more features than all other rank areas, making it completely worth purchasing!

PIG+ Zone Preview

What's Inside?

  • Exclusive Kits & Generators

    Access the element 118 (Oganesson) generator, along with a kit gen with 9 random kits, a gen with water and lava, and a few more!

  • Infinite Pigs & Race Track

    This place has an infinite pig spawner for both Cold and Warm pigs along with doubling as a pig racetrack to play with your friends. It can also be used as a PVP Arena with its huge size.

  • Ultimate Kits

    Down in the crator in the middle there is a room with exclusive kits which are better versions of God Kits called Ultimate Kits. There are versions of many kits including non-god kits which are also qualified as Ultimate Kits.

  • Much more

    There are even more features here that aren't listed in this page. Explore the area in game to find out what the rest are!

How to Obtain PIG+

PIG+ can be purchased in the Rank Shop. The entrance is located in the MVP++ Zone.
