Misc Areas

Explore the miscellaneous areas in the Puggy Realm! These areas are smaller or special locations without a larger sized portal dedicated to them.

Miscellaneous Portals

  • Maze

    Test your skills in a complex maze and reach the end for a money prize!

  • Park

    Relax with friends and enjoy various activities!

  • Parkour

    Challenge yourself with a variety of parkour courses! Complete them to win money and other random items.

  • Adventure Zone

    Go on an adventure in an open world styled area where you must fight mobs to gain prices such as money and kits!
